Earth's Natural Satellite

The Moon

Chandra · Luna · Selene

384,400 km from Earth
27.3 Earth days / orbit
3,474 km diameter
1/6 Earth's gravity
🌑

Formation

The Moon likely formed after a Mars-sized body collided with the early Earth, launching debris that eventually coalesced into the Moon.

🌕

Surface & Geology

The surface is covered by regolith, basaltic plains called maria, and many ancient impact craters that tell the story of early solar system history.

🌊

Tidal Influence

The Moon controls Earth tides and helps stabilize our planet's axial tilt, which contributes to a stable climate.

🚀

Exploration History

From early lunar probes to Apollo landings, the Moon has been humanity’s first destination beyond Earth.

🌡️

Atmosphere & Temperature

The Moon has an extremely thin exosphere and temperatures that swing from a scorching day to a freezing night.

🇮🇳

India & The Moon

India’s Chandrayaan missions have expanded our lunar knowledge and shown new capabilities in lunar exploration.
