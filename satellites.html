Upcoming ISRO Missions

2025–26

Gaganyaan

Human Spaceflight

India's first crewed orbital spacecraft. Will carry 3 Indian astronauts (Gagannauts) to a 400 km low Earth orbit for a 3-day mission. Uncrewed test flights (G1, G2) precede the crewed mission.

CrewedLEOHistoric
2027

Chandrayaan-4

Lunar Sample Return

A complex mission to land on the Moon, collect samples from the lunar surface, and return them safely to Earth — a technological feat achieved only by USA, USSR, and China so far.

MoonSample ReturnRobotic
2028

Shukrayaan-1

Venus Orbiter

India's first mission to Venus. The orbiter will study Venus's atmosphere, surface, and geology. Planned with a suite of scientific instruments to probe the dense Venusian clouds and understand planetary evolution.

VenusOrbiterAtmosphere
2030

Mangalyaan-2

Mars Orbiter Mission 2

The successor to India's successful MOM-1 (2013). Will carry advanced instruments for more detailed Martian atmosphere, mineral composition, and climate studies. May include a lander component.

MarsOrbiterScience
